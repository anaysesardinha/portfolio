# Picking Strategy Comparison

:::info Audience
This page is for Warehouse Operations staff picking orders under a
specific strategy.
:::

| Strategy | Full name | Picks based on | Best suited for |
|---|---|---|---|
| FIFO | First in, first out | Receipt date, oldest unit first | Non-perishable goods with no expiration concern |
| FEFO | First expired, first out | Expiration date, soonest first | Perishable or dated goods, such as food or pharmaceuticals |
| FMFO | First manufactured, first out | Manufacture date, oldest first | Goods that degrade with age but carry no printed expiration date |

Wayfinder WMS assigns the strategy per SKU based on the client contract.
Warehouse staff do not choose a strategy at pick time; the pick list
Wayfinder WMS generates already reflects the correct one.
