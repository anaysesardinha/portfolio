# Audience personas

:::info[Audience]
This page is for anyone writing or reviewing pages in this sample.
:::

This sample writes for three personas:

| Persona | Role summary | Primary need from these docs | Technical level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Warehouse Operations | Front-line staff running receiving, storage, picking, and cycle counts | Exact, repeatable procedures: SOPs and how-to guides | Comfortable with Wayfinder WMS day to day; no assumed logistics jargon beyond terms defined in context |
| Account Managers / Client Services | Client-facing staff managing contracts, SLAs, and escalations | Context for client conversations: explanation pages | Familiar with contract and SLA terms; limited exposure to warehouse-floor procedures |
| New Hires | Staff onboarding into any of the above roles | Background before task instructions: explanation pages first, then how-to guides | Limited familiarity with Meridian-specific systems and terminology |

## How this maps to content

The table above maps to three rules for writing content:

- Explanation pages assume a New Hire or Account Manager reading
  without prior context.
- How-to guides and SOPs assume a Warehouse Operations reader who
  already completed onboarding.
- Reference pages serve all three personas as lookup material, not as a
  starting point.
