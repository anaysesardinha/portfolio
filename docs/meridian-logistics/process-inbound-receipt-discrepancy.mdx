# Process an Inbound Receipt Discrepancy

:::info[Audience]
This page is for Warehouse Operations staff receiving inbound shipments.
:::

Use this guide when a shipment's count or condition does not match its
_ASN_. An ASN, or advance shipping notice, is the document a supplier
sends ahead of a shipment.

This guide assumes you already know the basic receiving flow in [The
Warehouse Lifecycle](/docs/meridian-logistics/warehouse-lifecycle).

## Steps

1. Stop put-away for the affected line items. Do not move mismatched
   units into storage until the discrepancy is logged. Moving them first
   makes the mismatch much harder to trace.
2. Open the shipment's receipt record in
   [Wayfinder WMS](/docs/meridian-logistics/reference/glossary).
3. Select **Log Discrepancy**. The system creates a discrepancy record
   linked to that ASN.
4. Recount the affected line items and enter the physical count. Wayfinder
   WMS calculates the variance automatically once you save the count.
5. Photograph any damaged units and attach the photos to the discrepancy
   record.

:::warning
Do not select **Damaged** until photos are attached. A damage claim
without visual evidence delays Client Services' resolution with the
supplier.
:::

6. Select a discrepancy reason from the dropdown:

   - **Short-ship**
   - **Over-ship**
   - **Damaged**
   - **Mislabeled**

   The discrepancy record moves to **Pending Review**.
7. Select **Notify** on the discrepancy record to notify the Client
   Services contact for that account. This creates a timestamped record
   that the client was informed. This record is separate from the
   internal discrepancy log.
8. Resume put-away for any unaffected line items on the same shipment.
   They do not need to wait on the discrepancy review.

## Expected outcome

The discrepancy record moves through Wayfinder WMS from **Pending
Review** to **Resolved**. This happens once Client Services confirms the
client's response. Affected units stay outside sellable inventory until
that happens.
