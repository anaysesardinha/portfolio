# Process an inbound receipt discrepancy

This page covers what to do when a shipment's actual count or
condition doesn't match what's stated on its advance shipping notice
(ASN), the document a supplier sends ahead of the shipment.

It assumes you already know the basic receiving flow in [The
warehouse lifecycle](/docs/meridian-logistics/warehouse-lifecycle).

## Prerequisites

Before you start, confirm you have:

- Access to view the shipment's receipt record in Wayfinder WMS,
  Meridian's warehouse management system
- Permission to select **Log Discrepancy** and **Notify** on a receipt
  record
- A camera or phone to photograph any damaged units

## Resolve the discrepancy

Follow these steps as soon as you notice the mismatch:

1. Stop put-away for the affected line items. Do not move mismatched
   units into storage until you log the discrepancy.
2. Open the shipment's receipt record in Wayfinder WMS.
3. Select **Log Discrepancy**. The system creates a discrepancy record
   and links it to that ASN.
4. Recount the affected line items and enter the physical count. Wayfinder
   WMS calculates the variance automatically after you save the count.
5. Photograph any damaged units and attach the photos to the discrepancy
   record.

6. Select a discrepancy reason from the dropdown:

   - **Short-ship**: a shipment that contains fewer units of a SKU
     than its paperwork lists.
   - **Over-ship**: a shipment that contains more units of a SKU than
     its paperwork lists.
   - **Damaged**: a received unit that arrives physically damaged.
   :::warning
   Do not select **Damaged** until you attach photos. A damage claim
   without visual evidence delays Client Services' resolution with the
   supplier.
   :::
   - **Mislabeled**: a received unit whose label does not match its
     actual SKU or contents.

   The discrepancy record moves to **Pending Review**.
7. Select **Notify** on the discrepancy record. This alerts the
   account's Client Services contact and timestamps that notification.
8. Resume put-away for any unaffected line items on the shipment; they
   don't need to wait on the review.

Once Client Services confirms the client's response, Wayfinder WMS
moves the record to **Resolved** and staff can put away the affected
units.
