# The Warehouse Lifecycle

:::info Audience
This page is for Warehouse Operations staff and new hires learning how
inventory moves through a Meridian site.
:::

Every unit that enters a Meridian warehouse passes through four stages.
Knowing this flow makes it easier to understand why any single procedure
exists: each one exists to move inventory safely from one stage to the
next.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Inbound receiving] --> B[Storage]
    B --> C[Order picking & pack-out]
    C --> D[Outbound distribution]
```

## Inbound receiving

A shipment arrives, gets checked against its paperwork, and gets logged
into Wayfinder WMS (Meridian's warehouse management system). Staff verify
quantity and condition before the shipment moves any further. A mismatch
here becomes a receiving discrepancy, handled through its own how-to
guide rather than worked around on the dock.

## Storage

Once received, inventory moves into one of three storage types:

- **Ambient** storage, for goods with no temperature requirement.
- **Climate-monitored** storage, for goods that need a controlled
  temperature range but not full cold-chain handling.
- **Bonded** storage, for goods held under customs control before duty is
  paid.

Which type a SKU (stock keeping unit, the identifier for a distinct
product) uses depends on the client contract and the product itself, not
on which space happens to be free that day.

## Order picking and pack-out

When an order comes in, Wayfinder WMS generates a pick list using the
picking strategy that SKU requires (FIFO, FEFO, or FMFO, compared in the
[picking strategy reference](/docs/meridian-logistics/reference/picking-strategy-comparison)).
Staff pick the items, pack them, and stage them for outbound.

## Outbound distribution

Packed orders are verified against the original order one more time, then
handed off to the outbound carrier. This final check is what catches a
pick-and-pack error before it becomes a customer-facing problem, which is
why it exists as its own step rather than an assumed part of packing.
