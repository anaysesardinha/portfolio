# Process an Inbound Receipt Discrepancy

:::info[Audience]
This page is for Warehouse Operations staff receiving inbound shipments.
:::

Use this guide when a received shipment's physical count or condition
does not match its _ASN_. An ASN, or advance shipping notice, is the
document a supplier sends ahead of a shipment.

This guide assumes you already know the basic receiving flow in [The
Warehouse Lifecycle](/docs/meridian-logistics/explanation/warehouse-lifecycle).

## Steps

1. Stop put-away for the affected line items. Do not move mismatched
   units into storage until the discrepancy is logged. Moving them first
   makes the mismatch much harder to trace.
2. Open the shipment's receipt record in
   [Wayfinder WMS](/docs/meridian-logistics/reference/glossary).
3. Select **Log Discrepancy**. The system creates a discrepancy record
   linked to that ASN.
4. Recount the affected line items and enter the physical count. Wayfinder
   WMS calculates the variance automatically once you save the count.
5. Photograph any damaged units and attach the photos to the discrepancy
   record.

:::warning
Select **Damaged** as the reason only after you attach photo evidence to
the discrepancy record. A damage claim without photos is far harder for
Client Services to resolve with the supplier.
:::

6. Select a discrepancy reason from the dropdown: **Short-ship**,
   **Over-ship**, **Damaged**, or **Mislabeled**. The discrepancy record
   moves to **Pending Review**.
7. Notify the Client Services contact for that account, using the
   discrepancy record's **Notify** button. This creates a timestamped
   record that the client was informed, separate from the internal
   discrepancy log.
8. Resume put-away for any line items on the same shipment that were not
   affected. They do not need to wait on the discrepancy review.

## Expected outcome

The discrepancy record moves through Wayfinder WMS from **Pending
Review** to **Resolved** once Client Services confirms the client's
response. Affected units stay outside sellable inventory until that
happens.
