# Escalate an Inventory Variance

:::info Audience
This page is for Warehouse Operations staff who found a variance during a
cycle count that survived a recount.
:::

Use this guide once a recount confirms a variance instead of resolving
it. Do not escalate before recounting; most variances are miscounts, not
real inventory errors. See [Run a Cycle
Count](/docs/meridian-logistics/how-to/run-a-cycle-count) for that step.

## Steps

1. Select **Escalate Variance** on the bin's cycle count record in
   Wayfinder WMS. This locks the bin from further picks until the
   variance is resolved. A locked bin prevents the variance from
   compounding while it is under review.
2. Enter the variance amount and the SKU affected. Wayfinder WMS
   pre-fills these from your cycle count, so confirm them rather than
   retyping them.
3. Check Wayfinder WMS for open transactions against that SKU in the
   last 24 hours (put-aways, picks, or transfers). Note anything you
   find in the escalation record.
4. Assign the escalation to your shift supervisor using the **Assign**
   field. Do not leave an escalation unassigned; an unassigned
   escalation has no owner until someone happens to notice it.
5. If the variance affects a client's sellable inventory count, notify
   the Client Services contact for that account. ClearLane Visibility
   updates the client-facing count only after the escalation is
   resolved, so a delay here does not risk showing the client incorrect
   numbers in the meantime.

## Expected outcome

The escalation record moves to **Under Review** in Wayfinder WMS, and the
affected bin stays locked until your supervisor resolves it and releases
the bin for picking again.
